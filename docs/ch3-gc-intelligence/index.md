# Chapter 3: GC Intelligence

## In 30 Seconds

General contractors are classified by labor model (merit-shop vs. union) and relationship status (active revenue vs. target). Prioritize merit-shop GCs where your certifications add value. **Part 8 research added Berghammer Construction and expanded pipelines for Moore, Stevens, McShane, and Greenfire — total new GC pipeline: $2.05M-$3.75M.**

## What to Say

"We're a certified SBE/DBE painting contractor. Our certifications can help you meet participation goals on City and County projects. We'd like to get on your bid list."

## Qualification Triggers

Before pursuing a GC relationship:

1. Confirm merit-shop status (ABC membership, website language, or direct inquiry)
2. Verify they work in our target segments
3. Check for active projects in our geography
4. Review prequalification requirements

## Risk Flags

1. Union signatory — do not pursue
2. History of payment issues — research before bidding
3. Exclusively public school work — likely union-dominated
