## Why Union GCs Are Problematic

1. **Job site conflicts** — Non-union subs face friction on union job sites
2. **Margin erosion** — Union environments often require premium pricing
3. **Preference hierarchy** — Union GCs prefer union subs
4. **PLA requirements** — Some projects require all-union labor
