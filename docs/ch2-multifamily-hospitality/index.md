# Chapter 2: Multifamily & Hospitality

## In 30 Seconds

Multifamily and hospitality represent the largest accessible market segment at ~$12M–$20M annually. Senior living developments use merit-shop GCs almost exclusively. WHEDA-funded housing has diversity requirements that favor DBE/SBE contractors.

**12-Month Pipeline: $2.4M–$4.2M**

---

## Key Markets

### Senior Living
- Merit-shop GC dominance
- Moore, VJS, McShane as primary builders
- Recurring work potential

### WHEDA-Funded Housing
- Diversity requirements favor DBE/SBE
- Cardinal Capital, MSP Real Estate

### Hospitality
- NCG Hospitality: 25% DBE requirement
- Moxy Milwaukee opportunity
