## Section 1.03 KPI Dictionary and Definitions

### Financial Metrics

| Term | Definition | Source |
|------|------------|--------|
| Revenue | Total contract value billed | QuickBooks Job P&L |
| COGS | Materials + labor + subs | QuickBooks Job P&L |
| Gross Profit | Revenue minus COGS | Calculated |
| Gross Margin % | Gross Profit ÷ Revenue | Calculated |
| Net Income | Gross Profit minus OpEx | QuickBooks |
| Net Margin % | Net Income ÷ Revenue | Calculated |

### GC Tier Definitions

| Tier | Margin Range | Action |
|------|--------------|--------|
| Strategic | >35% net margin | Expand aggressively |
| Growth | 20-35% net margin | Maintain and improve |
| Review | <20% net margin | Pricing discussion before next bid |
| Avoid | Loss or union | Do not bid |
