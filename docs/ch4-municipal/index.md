# Chapter 4: Municipal Markets

## In 30 Seconds

Municipal contracts offer certification-based competitive advantages. City of Milwaukee provides strongest advantage (25% SBE participation + 10% LBE bid preference). Milwaukee County recognizes DBE as TBE (25% goals).
