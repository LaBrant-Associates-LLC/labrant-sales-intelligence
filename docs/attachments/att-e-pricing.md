# Attachment E: Pricing Benchmarks

## Multifamily Pricing

| Scope | Range | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Per unit (turnkey) | $1,500-$2,500 | Depending on finishes |
| Common areas | 15-20% of unit total | Hallways, lobbies, amenities |
| Exterior | Project-specific | Siding, trim, balconies |

---

## Senior Living Pricing

| Scope | Range | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Per unit | $1,800-$3,000 | Higher finish standards |
| Common areas | 20-25% of unit total | More common space |

---

## Retail/Commercial Pricing

| Scope | Range | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Per SF (interior) | $0.75-$1.50 | Basic retail |
| Per SF (high-end) | $1.50-$3.00 | Specialty retail |

---

## Industrial Pricing

| Scope | Range | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Warehouse (basic) | $0.25-$0.50/SF | Minimal finish |
| Industrial coatings | $2.00-$5.00/SF | Specialty coatings |

---

## Specialty Pricing

| Scope | Range | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Church (sanctuary) | $3.00-$6.00/SF | High ceilings, specialty |
| Church (standard) | $1.50-$2.50/SF | Offices, classrooms |
| Self-storage | $25K-$75K/facility | Hallways, doors, office |
| Dealership | $15K-$100K/location | Service, showroom, exterior |

---

## Quick Reference

| Project Type | Rule of Thumb |
|--------------|---------------|
| Multifamily | $1,500-$2,500/unit |
| Senior Living | $1,800-$3,000/unit |
| Retail | $0.75-$3.00/SF |
| Church | $1.50-$6.00/SF |
| Self-storage | $25K-$75K/facility |
| Dealership | $15K-$100K/location |
