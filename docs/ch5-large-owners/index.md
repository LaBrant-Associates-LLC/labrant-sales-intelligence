# Chapter 5: Large Facilities Owners

## In 30 Seconds

Direct relationships with large property owners bypass GC markup and create recurring maintenance opportunities. Target owners with 10+ properties who value consistency and certification advantages.

**12-Month Pipeline: $400K–$1.2M**

---

## Target Owner Types

### Property Management Companies
- Multiple properties = recurring work
- Consistent specifications
- Relationship-based selection

### Retail Portfolios
- Scheels (Mayfair Mall): $400K–$800K opportunity
- Dealership networks
- Self-storage operators

### Developers (Direct)
- Sawall Development
- Capri Communities
- Avoid: Work typically flows through GCs

## Approach Strategy

1. Identify owners with 10+ properties in SE Wisconsin
2. Research their typical procurement process
