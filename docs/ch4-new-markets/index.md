# Chapter 4: New Markets

## In 30 Seconds

Emerging market segments with lower competition and certification advantages. Religious institutions represent 642+ congregations needing lead-certified contractors. Self-storage and industrial markets offer recurring work potential.

**12-Month Pipeline: $900K–$1.8M**

---

## Market Segments

### Religious Institutions
- 642+ congregations in SE Wisconsin
- Lead certification required (pre-1978 buildings)
- Low competition from certified contractors
- Archdiocese: 190+ parishes

### Self-Storage
- New construction: $25K–$75K per facility
- Hallways, doors, office areas
- Recurring maintenance potential

### Industrial/Manufacturing
- Warehouse and manufacturing facilities
- Specialty coatings opportunities
- $0.25–$5.00/SF depending on scope
