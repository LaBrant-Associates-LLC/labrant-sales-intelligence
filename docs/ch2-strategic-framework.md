# Chapter 2: Strategic Framework

## In 30 Seconds

LaBrant & Associates is a non-union commercial painting contractor in SE Wisconsin with approximately 20 employees and project capacity from $5K to $2M+. The certification portfolio creates structural advantages in markets with diversity participation requirements. **Current identified pipeline: $5.95M-$11.4M.**

---

## Section 2.01 Master Strategy Summary

### What to Say

"We're a certified SBE/DBE commercial painting contractor with 20 employees, bonding capacity to $5 million, and lead certification for pre-1978 work."

### Qualification Triggers

Before pursuing any opportunity:

1. Is the GC merit-shop? (Chapter 0 → GC Index)
2. Does the project have diversity goals we can satisfy?
3. Is the project type Tier 1 or Tier 2? (Section 2.02)
4. Is the project in our geographic scope?

## Section 2.03 Markets to Avoid

!!! danger "Do Not Pursue"

### (A) Union-Dominated Segments

| Segment | Why Avoid |
|---------|-----------|
| Public K-12 schools | JP Cullen, Miron, Findorff dominate |
| UW System projects | Prevailing wage, union preference |
| Major hospital systems | Aurora, Froedtert, Ascension, Children's |
| Convention centers | Union PLAs |

**Exception:** MPS cosmetic painting (direct contract) is Tier 1.

### (B) Data Centers (100% Union PLAs)

| Project | Investment | Union Status |
|---------|------------|--------------|
| Microsoft Mount Pleasant | $7.3B | 2,300 union jobs |
| Vantage/OpenAI Port Washington | $15B | Formal labor agreement |
| Microsoft Caledonia | $7B+ | Expected union PLA |
| Meta Beaver Dam | $500M | Expected union PLA |

**Do not pursue data center construction.**

### (C) Fire Hydrant Painting

20 of 21 utilities surveyed do hydrant painting in-house. Only Oconomowoc contracts out.

**Do not pursue fire hydrant painting as a market.**
